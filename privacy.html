Orveno Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Orveno ("Orveno", "we", "us", "our"). Orveno is a short-video app where you can watch, create and share videos and photos. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. By using Orveno you confirm that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the app.

2. Who can use Orveno

Orveno is for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete that account and its data. If you are under the age of majority in your country, please use the app with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.

3. Information we collect

Account information: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.

Profile information: the username, display name, bio, profile photo, cover photo, country or region and phone number (only if you choose to add it) that you enter in your profile.

Content you create: videos, photos, captions, hashtags, comments, sounds and effects that you record, upload or post, together with the privacy setting you choose for each post.

Activity in the app: likes, saves, follows, shares, comments, views, searches you make inside the app and the accounts you block or report.

Messages: messages you send and receive in the app's chat feature.

Earnings and payout information: if you use Monetization, your earnings, wallet balance, withdrawal requests and the payout details you enter to receive a withdrawal.

Device and technical information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, network status, crash and performance information, a notification token so we can send you notifications, and your advertising ID.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts or your SMS messages.

4. Permissions the app asks for

Camera and microphone: only when you record a video or take a photo inside the app.

Photos and videos (media access): only when you choose to upload existing photos or videos from your device.

Notifications: to tell you about likes, comments, followers, messages and account activity. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Network access and network state: to load videos and to tell you when you are offline.

Foreground service: to keep an upload running while the app is in the background.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings. Features that need that permission will then stop working, but the rest of the app will continue to work.

5. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To show you videos and photos, and to personalize your feed and search results.
  • To let you upload, post, comment, follow, message and interact with other people.
  • To run Monetization, calculate earnings and process withdrawal requests.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.
  • To show advertisements and measure how they perform.
  • To keep the app safe: detect spam, fraud, abuse and violations of our rules, and to review reports.
  • To fix problems, improve performance and develop new features.
  • To comply with the law.

6. Advertising

Orveno shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your advertising ID, device information and information about how you interact with ads. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your choices. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device's Google settings (Settings > Google > Ads). How Google uses data is described in the Google Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not sell your personal information.

7. What other people can see and how we share information

Your username, display name, profile photo, bio and the posts you set to be public can be seen by other people. If you set your account or a post to private, only the people you allow can see it. Your likes, comments and follows may be visible to others according to your settings. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation.

We share information only in these cases: with service providers who help us run the app (for example servers and hosting, ad services and notification delivery) and who may use the information only to provide those services to us; when the law requires it or to respond to valid legal requests; to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or Orveno; and with your consent. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

8. Storage and security

Your information is stored on our servers. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) when data moves between your device and our servers, access controls, and a requirement that you unlock your phone with your screen lock before opening your wallet or requesting a withdrawal. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your Google account secure.

9. Data retention and account deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account at any time from the account settings inside the app. When you permanently delete your account, your data is deleted automatically from our servers, including your profile, videos, photos, comments, likes, saves, follows, messages and wallet information. Deletion is automatic and you do not need to contact us. Copies that exist in temporary caches or routine backups are removed within a short time, and we may keep only the limited information that the law requires us to keep, such as records needed for tax, accounting or fraud prevention. Content that other people have already saved or shared outside the app is not under our control.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile information at any time.
  • Make your account private and choose who can see each post.
  • Delete your own posts and comments.
  • Block or report people and content.
  • Turn off notifications and remove permissions in your device settings.
  • Delete your account and data as described above.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or take a copy of your information, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Most of these actions are available directly in the app.

11. International use

Our servers may be located in a different country from yours. Your information may be processed and stored there, and we protect it as described in this policy wherever it is processed.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make important changes we will show you the updated policy in the app. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Using the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
