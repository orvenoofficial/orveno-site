Delete your Orveno account

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Orveno account and the data linked to it. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Orveno and go to your Profile.
  2. Open the menu and choose Settings and privacy.
  3. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete account permanently, choose a reason and confirm.

What is deleted

When you permanently delete your account, your data is deleted automatically from our servers. This includes your profile, videos, photos, comments, likes, saves, follows, messages and wallet information.

Copies in temporary caches or routine backups are removed within a short time. We may keep only the limited information that the law requires us to keep, such as records needed for tax, accounting or fraud prevention.

If you cannot open the app

Email us from the email address linked to your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify the request and delete the account.
